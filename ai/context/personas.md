# Personas

## Existing v1 developer

Uses the Markdown-based Empirical SDD scaffold today. Wants automatic resume
and trustworthy completion without renaming or rebuilding existing specs.

## Fast-moving developer

Ships small, low-risk changes frequently. Needs the workflow to add confidence
without turning a short edit into a long ceremony.

## High-assurance developer

Works on substantial, risky, or user-visible changes. Needs explicit design,
planning, tests, visual evidence, independent review, and controlled delivery.

## Tool integrator

Connects an IDE, agent, or automation service to the protocol. Needs stable
schemas and APIs without depending on another product's database or runtime.
